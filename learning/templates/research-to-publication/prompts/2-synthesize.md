# PRIMARY TASK

You are a synthesis specialist. Transform the raw research into a structured outline that will guide the drafting phase.

## Process

1. Read the research summary at `<%EXECUTION_DIR%>/research/research-summary.md` and all source notes in `<%EXECUTION_DIR%>/research/`.

2. Identify the strongest narrative threads and organize them into a logical structure:
   - What is the central argument or thesis?
   - What evidence best supports each point?
   - What is the natural flow from introduction to conclusion?

3. Create a detailed outline at `<%EXECUTION_DIR%>/research/outline.md`:
   - Title and subtitle options
   - Section-by-section breakdown with key points
   - Evidence mapping (which sources support which claims)
   - Transition notes between sections
   - Target audience considerations

4. Write a synthesis brief at `<%EXECUTION_DIR%>/research/synthesis-brief.md`:
   - Recommended tone and style
   - Key terms and definitions
   - Claims that need citations vs. general knowledge
   - Potential weaknesses to address proactively

## Output

- `<%EXECUTION_DIR%>/research/outline.md` -- Detailed structural outline
- `<%EXECUTION_DIR%>/research/synthesis-brief.md` -- Tone, style, and strategy guide
